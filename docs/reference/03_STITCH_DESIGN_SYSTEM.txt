## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Core Philosophy
The design system embodies an honest, earth-rooted, and caring aesthetic tailored for modern quick-commerce grocery delivery. The emotional target is domestic warmth, uncompromised freshness, and rural transparency translated into high-efficiency mobile utility. The interface rejects synthetic hyper-bright delivery app visual noise in favor of an artisanal, farm-to-table editorial language.

### Visual Style Direction: Editorial Tactile & Warm Naturalism
This system couples tactile agricultural warmth with structured, rapid-commerce clarity:
- **Serif Editorial Anchors**: High-character serif headlines grounded by legible, traditional body serif typography cultivate literary trust, reminiscent of heirloom seed packets and heritage estate pantries.
- **Surface-Driven Warmth**: A generous cream canvas replaces clinical stark white, punctuated by crisp white product containers and pastoral leafy accents.
- **Restrained Materiality**: Natural tactile structure supported by delicate vegetable-tanned borderlines, avoiding heavy synthetic skeuomorphism, noisy neo-brutalism, or glassy blurs.

## Colors

### Hierarchy & Chromatic Roles
- **Primary (`#234C36` - Forest Green)**: The anchor of clarity and intent. Reserved for primary conversion triggers (CTAs, stepper increments), active bottom tab navigation states, critical headers, and high-priority icon markers.
- **Secondary (`#EEE1A6` - Harvest Yellow)**: The seasonal and promotional accent. Applied to badge containers, organic certification stamps, celebratory promotion cards, and harvest banners.
- **Tertiary (`#D9FAAF` - Lime Green)**: Interactive spark. Used strictly in sparing micro-doses—selected pill chips, interactive flash deals, and novelty tags.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF2E6` - Cream)**: The warm backdrop for all application views, conveying natural parchment, unbleached grain, and daylight tranquility.

### Contextual Color Roles
- **`soft-green` (`#DBF5D1`)**: Used for background tiers of benefit summaries, reassurance banners, and highlighted comparison columns.
- **`deep-text` (`#243126`)**: The single primary reading color for maximum contrast and readability against cream and white surfaces without the starkness of pure black.
- **`muted-text` (`#706F63`)**: Applied to secondary metadata, weights, pack sizes, and timestamp captions.
- **`white-surface` (`#FFFFFF`)**: Strictly designated for product cards, bottom sheets, form inputs, and checkout surfaces.
- **`border-subtle` (`#E5DDCF`)**: Hairline delineation across cards, tables, search containers, and dividers.
- **`error-alert` (`#B42318`)**: Critical inventory warnings, out-of-stock badges, form errors, and checkout failure toasts.

## Typography

### Structural Decisions
- **Display & Headlines (`Literata`)**: Conveys physical craft and botanical authenticity. Used for banner slogans, category banners, product names on detail views, and high-impact headlines.
- **Editorial Body & Labels (`Source Serif 4`)**: Selected for high-density rendering on retina displays, preserving readability in tabular product specs, ingredient breakdowns, delivery instructions, and micro-labels.
- **Numeric Figures & Prices**: Render tabular figures in `Source Serif 4` at weight 600 or 700 to ensure clear vertical alignment in receipt summaries and checkout calculations.
- **Case Formatting**: Never use all-caps for body copy or card titles. Sentence case maintains an honest, conversational, and welcoming tone.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Architecture
- **Mobile First Framework (360px – 480px)**: 
  - Standard edge canvas margin is `1rem` (16px).
  - Main product grid: 2 columns with `0.75rem` (12px) horizontal and vertical gaps.
  - Horizontal carousel rows display a `16px` right-edge peek on subsequent cards to signal swipeability.
  - Value propositions: 2×2 grid layout with `0.5rem` internal item spacing.
  - Bottom navigation fixed at viewport base with 64px structural height + safe-area insets.
- **Tablet (481px – 1024px)**:
  - Margins expand to `1.5rem` (24px).
  - Product grids scale dynamically to 3 or 4 columns.
  - Value proposition blocks shift into a single 4-card horizontal banner row.
  - Modals convert to docked side drawers or centered floating dialogs.
- **Desktop (1025px+)**:
  - Max canvas container constrained to `1200px` centered.
  - Standard 12-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters.
  - PDP (Product Detail Page) employs a dedicated 2-column layout: Sticky product media presentation (7 columns) and purchasing meta-flow (5 columns).
  - Bottom bar collapses in favor of a fixed utility header.

## Elevation & Depth

### Surface Tiers & Flat Depth Philosophy
The design avoids deep drop shadows, heavy glassmorphism, or synthetic blurs. Depth is communicated strictly via tonal surface layering and low-contrast borders:

1. **Base Layer (`#FAF2E6` - Cream)**: The canvas upon which the entire interface rests.
2. **Container Tier (`#FFFFFF` - White Surface)**: Clean cards, search modules, and inputs sit on the cream canvas with an explicit 1px outline of `#E5DDCF`.
3. **Accent Highlight Layer (`#DBF5D1` & `#EEE1A6`)**: Inset containers for key product traits, certifications, and delivery alerts sit inside white cards or directly on cream.
4. **Interactive Hover Elevation (Desktop Only)**: A delicate, warm ambient shadow:
   - `box-shadow: 0 4px 12px rgba(36, 49, 38, 0.06);` with a border color transition to `#234C36`.
5. **Overlay Tier (Bottom Sheets, Menus)**:
   - Backdrop scrim: `rgba(36, 49, 38, 0.4)` (deep forest charcoal tint).
   - Elevated sheet surface: Solid `#FFFFFF` anchored with top borders rounded at `1rem`. No background blur is applied.

## Shapes

### Geometry & Radius Rules
- **Base Components (Rounded Level 2 - `0.5rem` / `8px` to `1rem` / `16px`)**:
  - Product Cards & Value Prop Cards: `1rem` (16px).
  - Form Fields, Search Bars, Quantity Steppers: `0.75rem` (12px).
  - Dialogs & Bottom Sheet Tops: `1rem` (16px).
- **Pill Shape (`9999px`)**:
  - Filter chips, dietary requirement badges (e.g., "100% Organic", "Cold Pressed"), cart count badges, and sub-category quick navigation pills.
- **Borders & Dividers**:
  - Consistent hairline width: `1px solid #E5DDCF`.
  - Nested media: Produce photos within product cards share a top border radius of `0.75rem` (12px), aligning cleanly within the parent card container.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (CTA)**:
  - Background: `#234C36` (Forest Green), Text: `#FFFFFF`, Radius: `0.75rem` (12px).
  - Padding: `0.75rem 1.5rem`, Font: `label-lg`.
  - Active/Pressed: Darkens by 10% brightness. Focus ring: `2px solid #234C36` with 2px offset.
- **Secondary Action**:
  - Background: `#FAF2E6`, Border: `1px solid #234C36`, Text: `#234C36`, Radius: `0.75rem` (12px).
- **Tertiary / Ghost Button**:
  - Transparent background, Text: `#234C36`, subtle underline on hover.

### Product Cards
- Surface: `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid #E5DDCF` border, Radius: `1rem`.
- Media Aspect Ratio: `1:1` square, set against soft natural daylight backgrounds with zero clipping.
- Content Stack:
  - Sub-label: `#706F63` (`label-md`) indicating weight/pack size (e.g., `500 g`, `1 bunch`).
  - Title: `#243126` (`title-card`).
  - Price: Tabular figures in `#234C36` (`headline-sm`).
  - Action: Bottom right "+" or quantity stepper embedded inside the card boundary.

### Chips & Badges
- **Tag / Category Chip (Unselected)**:
  - Background: `#FFFFFF`, Border: `1px solid #E5DDCF`, Text: `#706F63`, Radius: `9999px` (Pill).
- **Tag / Category Chip (Selected)**:
  - Background: `#DBF5D1`, Border: `1px solid #234C36`, Text: `#234C36`, Radius: `9999px` (Pill).
- **Promotional Badge**:
  - Background: `#EEE1A6`, Text: `#243126`, Radius: `9999px`.
- **Accent Micro-Badge**:
  - Background: `#D9FAAF`, Text: `#234C36`, Radius: `9999px`.

### Quantity Stepper
- Compact inline component: 32px height.
- Background: `#FAF2E6`, Border: `1px solid #E5DDCF`, Radius: `0.5rem`.
- Decrement/Increment triggers: `#234C36` with a central numeric counter rendered in `label-lg`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Background: `#FFFFFF`, Border: `1px solid #E5DDCF`, Radius: `0.75rem`, Text: `#243126`.
- Placeholder text: `#706F63`.
- Active Focus State: Border switches directly to `1.5px solid #234C36`.
- Error State: Border switches to `1.5px solid #B42318` accompanied by helper text in `label-sm`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size: 20px × 20px.
- Unchecked: Border `1.5px solid #E5DDCF`, background `#FFFFFF`.
- Checked: Border `1.5px solid #234C36`, background `#234C36`, check mark icon `#FFFFFF`.
- Radio retains concentric circle geometry; checkbox retains soft `4px` radius.

### Mobile Bottom Navigation Bar
- Fixed base position with background `#FFFFFF` and top hairline border `1px solid #E5DDCF`.
- Height: 64px + safe area padding.
- Unselected tab icon & label: `#706F63`.
- Active tab icon & label: `#234C36` with a micro underline indicator dot (`#234C36`).
- Icons: Crisp 1.5px outline style across Home, Categories, Search, Orders, and Profile.

### Sticky Purchase Drawer / Checkout Bar
- Background: `#FFFFFF`, Border-top: `1px solid #E5DDCF`.
- Split distribution: Left side contains total checkout sum and delivery ETA; right side houses the primary conversion CTA button.
